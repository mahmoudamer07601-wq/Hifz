The translations of the meanings shown in this app (a touch on an ayah, or the mouse resting on it)
come from Tanzil.net (https://tanzil.net/trans/), copied unchanged:

  en  Saheeh International (Tanzil id en.sahih)
  es  Julio Cortés (Tanzil id es.cortes)

Tanzil distributes them for non-commercial use, with the source shown and a link to its translations
page so that readers can follow its corrections. Where each copy in the kit comes from:
